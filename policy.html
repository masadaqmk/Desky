Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Desky (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a premium Android charging dock display. This policy explains what information the app may access, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Desky is designed to run primarily on your device. Most settings, themes, and preferences are stored locally on your phone and are not transmitted to us.

  • Device permissions — The app may request access to features you enable, such as location (for weather), calendar, photos, sensors, or storage, depending on the widgets and StandBy features you use.
  • Weather data — When you enable weather on StandBy, approximate location or a location you provide may be sent to third-party weather APIs to retrieve forecast data. We do not sell this information.
  • Diagnostics — If you contact us for support, we may receive information you choose to share (such as app version or a description of the issue).

How we use information

We use information only to provide app functionality you request, including:

  • Showing clocks, widgets, weather, calendar events, and media on your charging display
  • Remembering your theme, layout, and personalization choices on your device
  • Improving reliability and responding to support requests

Third-party services

Weather and other optional features may rely on third-party APIs. Those providers process requests according to their own privacy policies. Location is only used when you enable weather on StandBy.

Data retention

Preferences stored on your device remain until you change them or uninstall the app. We do not operate a central account system for Desky at this time.

Your choices

  • You can disable permissions or weather features at any time in the app or Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling Desky removes locally stored app data from your device.

Children’s privacy

Desky is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit desky.app or contact us through the support channel listed in the app store listing.
